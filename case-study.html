AI Contact Classification Pipeline

An automated pipeline that categorized 90,000+ business contacts using AI — replacing weeks of manual sorting with a process that runs in hours.

Problem
A database of 90,000+ contacts had names and emails but no categorization — making it impossible to segment or target outreach effectively.
Solution
A Python pipeline that sends each contact to an AI model and assigns a type, subtype, and industry based on their email, company, and role.
Outcome
Every contact fully categorized and ready for CRM import. The process is repeatable — it re-runs on new contacts anytime to keep the data clean.
